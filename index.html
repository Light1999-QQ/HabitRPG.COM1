THE PROJECT

About Habit RPG

A productivity experiment that turns useful everyday actions into a visible RPG progression system.

H

Make consistency visible.

Habit RPG connects simple tasks with XP, ranks, streaks, statistics and a simulated discipline arena. The idea is not to make a person work endlessly. The idea is to make useful actions easier to see and review.

SYSTEM RULES

What keeps the game fair?

01

Global daily reward limit prevents endless XP farming.

02

Each quest has its own completion limit for the day.

03

Higher levels require progressively more XP.

04

Bots are simulated and do not represent real users.

♥
OUR GOAL

Move people in a better direction.

The project is built around small, concrete actions: learn something, finish a task, organize a space, plan the next day and review what happened. The interface adds game feedback without pretending that XP itself is the goal.

01

Clarity

Tasks should be specific enough that you know exactly what “done” means.

02

Consistency

Streaks reward returning to useful actions over time.

03

Feedback

Statistics make progress easier to inspect instead of guessing.

04

Balance

Limits prevent the game layer from becoming a reason to click endlessly.
